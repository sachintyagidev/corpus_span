Historical Context (Nifty 50 / Sensex 1991-2024)

The bootstrap methods draw from these actual annual returns:

DecadeNifty CAGRAvg InflationReal ReturnBest Nifty YearWorst Nifty YearCharacter
1990s (91-99)17.8%~9.6%~7.5%+82% (1991)-21% (1995)Liberalization + high inflation
2000s (00-09)13.3%~5.6%~7.3%+76% (2009)-52% (2008)Dotcom + GFC + moderate CPI
2010s (10-19)8.9%~6.9%~1.9%+31% (2014)-25% (2011)Slow equity + sticky CPI
2020s (20-24)14.2%~5.8%~7.9%+24% (2021)+4% (2022)Post-COVID recovery

Paired Inflation approach: when the simulation samples Nifty 1995's -21%, it also uses 1995's 10.2% inflation. This preserves the real correlation between equity returns and inflation and avoids the unrealistic mix of worst-case equity + worst-case inflation that happens when you pick them independently.
